This study aims to model geoid surface in Lagos State Nigeria with polynomial models and artificial neural networks, and compare their accuracies. Performance of these models to optimally represent the geoid in the study area will be investigated. The results shall be compared with global geoid model EGM08, and the enrichment of the global geoid (that is, reducing it to fit the local geoid surface) shall be attempted. Thus, the difference between the zero reference surface for the determination of the orthometric heights in Lagos State in relation to the zero geoid surface of the EGM08 shall be determined.
